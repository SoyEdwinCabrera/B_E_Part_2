# Story Summary:

Lola visits Banco Cantábrico for her sales meeting and discovers that dark forces are working against her. Who is trying to discredit her? And how can she protect herself? ?

## Key Learning Points:

We explain the grammar of numbers more specifically how to use COMPOUND NUMBER ADJECTIVES and how to express ORDINAL NUMBERS. We talk about business lunches and how you order a steak in a New York restaurant. Would you prefer it WELL DONE, MEDIUM, RARE or BLUE? And finally, we explain the usage of a couple of everyday expressions. How does it feel to be IN THE MOMENT? What do you need to do to CLOSE THE DEAL?
