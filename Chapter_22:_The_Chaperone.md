# Story Summary:

A nervous Park goes on his first date with the Girl with the Almond Eyes. He has very little experience in the world of romance. Will the date be a success or a disaster?

## Key Learning Points:

In this chapter we discuss CORRUPTION around the world using the work of Transparency International and their Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI). We talk about BRIBERY, BLACK MARKETS and THE SECRET POLICE. We also have three really useful common expressions. Nobody should believe that they are ABOVE THE LAW. Never TURN A BLIND EYE to criminal activity in your company. Always WEIGH UP THE PROS AND CONS when making an important decision.
