# Story Summary:

Lola returns to the office to find JBlock in a state of chaos because their systems have just been hacked by a mysterious intruder. How will the company react to this existential threat?

## Key Learning Points:

Let’s review four types of IMPERATIVES - the direct, polite, informal, and the royal! Let’s also discuss some interesting expressions. Do you think that one day you may become a DIGITAL NOMAD? Will that help you avoid having a NERVOUS BREAKDOWN? We take another look at the rhetorical devices we introduced in Chapter 12. How the RULE OF THREE helps you structure your arguments. And how conscious REPETITION makes your speech more memorable. And finally we ask the question - why do men love to give each other NICKNAMES?
