# Story Summary:

Park is concerned that the authorities suspect him of treason. He tries to focus on his work, but he is under increasing pressure from his supervisor, Kang.

## Key Learning Points:

We review the pronunciation of the “I” VOWEL in words such as a “priority”, “Wi-Fi” and “bilingual”. We also review the pronunciation of the “U” VOWEL in words such as “vulnerability”, “multinational” and “publicity”. We cover a couple of items of advanced vocabulary, namely the verb TO EXPEDITE and the noun SCENARIO. Then we discuss what happens in the event of a BLACK SKY EVENT. We explain how companies use an UNINTERRUPTED POWER SUPPLY and an AUTOMATIC TRANSFER SWITCH to guarantee the continuous supply of electricity to their offices. Finally, we explain the concept of a FIGHT-OR-FLIGHT response, something that everybody feels when they are in imminent danger.
