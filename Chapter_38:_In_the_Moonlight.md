# Story Summary:

Park reveals how he truly feels to the Girl with The Almond Eyes. He opens his heart to her. He tells her his dreams and desires. And then he awaits her reaction.

## Key Learning Points:

We introduce three new phrasal verbs - TO LEAN FORWARDS, TO SNATCH BACK and TO DUCK DOWN, referencing the book “Lean In” by Sheryl Sandberg We cover three colourful expressions. What makes your BLOOD RUN COLD? How do you keep your EYE ON THE BALL at work? How much is of your business is generated through WORD-OF-MOUTH? We have a brief discussion of the fundamental concepts of CAPITALISM and COMMUNISM. How do you define these terms? In this section we refer to the book “23 things they don’t tell you about Capitalism” by Ha Joon Chang And finally, with the help of Transparency International, we investigate CORRUPTION and ask the questions - which are the most corrupt countries in the world, and which are the least corrupt?
