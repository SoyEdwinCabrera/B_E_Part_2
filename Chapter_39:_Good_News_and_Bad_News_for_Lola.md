# Story Summary:

Back in Madrid, Lola visits the law offices of Gonzalo Garrido and receives some disturbing news. Her enemies are determined to ruin her past, present and future.

## Key Learning Points:

In this chapter we cover a lot of fundamental financial concepts and vocabulary. We start off by defining what ASSETS and LIABILITIES are. Then we explain what OPERATING PROFITS are and how they are used to calculate the ENTERPRISE VALUE of a company. And finally, we talk about SILENT PARTNERS and STAKEHOLDERS to whom the management ultimately answer. We also briefly discuss some legal English, such as the difference between a CIVIL COURT and a CRIMINAL COURT and what happens when you are SUED for DAMAGES. And finally, we explain some common idioms and expressions including BIGGER FISH TO FRY, SNAKE IN THE GRASS and GODSEND.
