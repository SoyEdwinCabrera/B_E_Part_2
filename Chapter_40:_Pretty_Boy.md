# Story Summary:

Cayetano Tolosa secretly listens to a private conversation between JJ and Diego as they contemplate the company’s future. What does he plan to do with this confidential information?

## Key Learning Points:

We continue to discuss fundamental business concepts. We look at a company’s different INCOME STREAMS. We explain the process of CASH BURN and why it is so significant for start-up companies. We include another three phrasal verbs, more specifically TO RUN OUT, TO SELL OUT and TO FLICK THROUGH. We discuss an everyday expression TO THINK ON YOUR FEET. And we consider the use of the word GOD DAMN. Is it ever appropriate to use words that could cause offence, in a business scenario? Finally, we do a brief review of the main RELIGIONS OF THE WORLD, the key vocabulary and how to pronounce the words.
